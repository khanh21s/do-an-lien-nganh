# T12 report: Subscription, pricing & Admin frontend

Branch `feat/T12-sub-admin-fe` (base 5faa51e = tip of T06). Spec: SUB-03, SUB-06, SUB-07, ADM-01..06 (FE), ADM-09.

## What was done
- **Pricing (`app/pricing/page.tsx`)**: renders page content only, with no `<main>` and no own logo/header. The shell comes from T07's `app/pricing/layout.tsx`. Plans come from `GET /subscriptions/plans`, sorted by duration and priced with `formatVnd` (`79.000đ`, `169.000đ`, `499.000đ`). The cheapest per-month plan gets a "Tiết kiệm nhất" badge. A Free vs Premium table uses static Free rows plus the union of `plan.features`. The buy button sends guests to `/login?next=%2Fsubscription`. The page says "Thanh toán 1 lần, không tự gia hạn" and has no Lifetime plan or `autoRenew`.
- **`/subscription`**: shows the status card with the end date and days left, the plan cards (VNPay) and the activation-code form. Transaction history is paginated.
  - createPayment **409** shows: "Bạn đang có gói Premium còn hạn… dùng mã kích hoạt…". It also refetches the status and scrolls to the code form. Pay buttons are disabled while the user is premium. **503** says VNPay is not configured.
  - Activation errors show the BE `error` text exactly as returned (inline and as a toast). On success the page invalidates `userSubscription`, `profile` and `transactions`.
- **`/subscription/success`**: polls `getUserSubscription` every 2s for up to 10s. Then it shows either "đã kích hoạt đến dd/mm/yyyy" or "Đang xử lý…" with a "Kiểm tra lại" button. Guests get a login link with `next`. When the subscription is active it invalidates `profile`.
- **`/subscription/failed`**: adds the T05 reason `invalid_amount`, plus "Thử lại" and "Xem bảng giá".
- **Admin guard (`app/(app)/admin/layout.tsx`)**: reads the shared `qk.profile`, shows a spinner while loading and ErrorState on error. A non-admin gets one toast, then `router.replace('/dashboard')`, and no admin UI is rendered.
- **Admin lessons**:
  - The list uses `Pagination`, ErrorState and an empty state. The create form uses `TOPIC_TAGS` and the imported `CEFR_LEVELS`, and delete goes through `ConfirmModal`.
  - The detail page has an info form for `UpdateLessonInput`. Unknown legacy topic tags stay selectable.
  - Audio upload is checked on the client: `audio/*` plus an extension of mp3/m4a/wav/ogg/webm, at most 50 MB. BE errors are shown as returned.
  - The sentence list has an edit modal for text, ipa, translationVi, startMs, endMs, sentenceOrder and audioUrl, and a confirmed delete (the BE renumbers). ErrorState covers 404 and other errors (ADM-09).
  - Every change invalidates `adminLessons`, `lessons`, `lesson-search` and `lesson(id)`.
- **Admin vocabulary**:
  - The deck list is paginated and links to `/admin/vocabulary/[deckId]`.
  - The deck page has search (debounced `searchWord`), pagination, and add/edit/delete with every field: partOfSpeech, ipa, example, exampleVi, audioUrl, imageUrl. The word itself is read-only when editing, because the BE `UpdateCardRequest` has no `word`.
- **`/admin/activation-codes`**:
  - The batch form has plan, quantity 1–500 and an optional date. With no date the BE defaults to +365 days.
  - The new batch panel offers copy each, copy all and CSV export (header + N rows, UTF-8 BOM).
  - The list has filters for all / unused (`used=false`) / used (`used=true`, with email and date), pagination, and per-page copy and CSV.
- **Tests (vitest)**:
  - `components/subscription/format.test.ts`: VND formatting, durations, best value, features.
  - `components/admin/admin-helpers.test.ts`: audio validation (20 MB mp3 accepted, `.exe` and >50 MB blocked), sentence and batch validation, CSV escaping, 10 codes giving 11 lines.
- **Shared files** (inside the `T12` blocks only): `types.ts` gets `DeckCardsParams`, and `endpoints/vocabulary.ts` gets `searchDeckCards`.
